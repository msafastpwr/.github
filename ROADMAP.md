# Annual Strategic Roadmap & OKRs (2026–2027)

**Microsoft Student Ambassadors — FAST-NUCES Peshawar**  
*Vision, Strategic Pillars, Quarterly Milestones, & Key Performance Indicators*

---

## 🧭 Executive Vision

For the **2026–2027 academic tenure**, Microsoft Student Ambassadors (FAST-NUCES Peshawar) is committed to transitioning from a traditional student society into a **production-grade engineering incubator**. Our goal is to ensure that every active student in our community gains real-world experience building, deploying, and collaborating on modern cloud and artificial intelligence systems.

---

## 🎯 Chapter Objectives & Key Results (OKRs)

### Objective 1: Accelerate Hands-On Cloud & AI Literacy
- **KR 1.1:** Guide **200+ FAST Peshawar students** through activating and utilizing their **Microsoft Azure for Students (\$100 free credit)** accounts.
- **KR 1.2:** Deliver **6 hands-on workshops** covering Azure Serverless, Azure OpenAI, Copilot Studio, and Git/GitHub.
- **KR 1.3:** Achieve at least **50 completed Microsoft Learn learning paths** or certification vouchers among active community members.

### Objective 2: Foster Production Open-Source Engineering
- **KR 2.1:** Launch and actively maintain the official chapter website portal ([`msafastpwr.github.io`](https://msafastpwr.github.io)) with zero downtime and automated CI/CD.
- **KR 2.2:** Scaffold and release **public workshop starter kits and lab repositories** on GitHub for 100% of technical events.
- **KR 2.3:** Onboard at least **30 first-time open-source contributors** to chapter repositories via `good first issue` workflows.

### Objective 3: Champion Diversity & Inclusion via Her Tech Wing
- **KR 3.1:** Onboard at least **40 female undergraduates** into the **Her Tech Wing** study circles and coding jams.
- **KR 3.2:** Organize a dedicated **Women in Tech Leadership Summit** featuring senior female engineers from Microsoft MVPs, alumni, and top industry firms.
- **KR 3.3:** Ensure at least **40% female representation** across executive leadership and departmental core bodies.

### Objective 4: Incubate Teams for Flagship Competitions & Hackathons
- **KR 4.1:** Form and mentor at least **4 student teams** submitting working prototypes to the global **Microsoft Imagine Cup 2027**.
- **KR 4.2:** Host a premier 24-hour university hackathon on the FAST Peshawar campus with industry sponsors.
