# Her Tech Wing — Charter & Inclusion Framework

**Microsoft Student Ambassadors — FAST-NUCES Peshawar**  
*Empowering, Mentoring, and Elevating Women in Computing & Engineering*

---

## 🌸 Preamble & Mission

In Pakistan's rapidly expanding technology sector, female representation in software development, cloud architecture, and engineering leadership remains disproportionately low. While computer science programs enroll talented female students, systemic barriers—such as intimidation in male-dominated lab environments, lack of visible female technical role models, and limited peer networks—often inhibit female undergraduates from participating in hackathons, open-source projects, and technical competitions.

**Her Tech Wing** was established as an autonomous, core operational wing within **Microsoft Student Ambassadors, FAST-NUCES Peshawar** to dismantle these barriers.

### Mission Statement
To create an inclusive, rigorous, and supportive environment where female engineering and computing students at FAST-NUCES Peshawar develop production-grade technical skills, build confidence in cloud and artificial intelligence systems, and emerge as trailblazing leaders in the global technology landscape.

### 1. Skill Acceleration & Cloud Bootcamps
- **Hands-on Microsoft Azure Training:** Focused workshops designed to help female undergraduates deploy their first cloud workloads, serverless APIs, and AI models.
- **Microsoft Learn Certification Sprints:** Structured group study paths preparing students for globally recognized certifications such as **Azure Fundamentals (AZ-900)** and **AI Fundamentals (AI-900)**.
- **Git & GitHub Mastery:** Demystifying pull requests, merge conflict resolution, and open-source contribution in low-pressure settings.

### 2. Peer Coding Circles & "Debug Without Fear" Jams
- Bi-weekly informal study jams where female freshmen and sophomores bring coursework, programming assignments, and bug traces for collaborative peer debugging.
- Ego-free learning: Every question is treated with respect. Senior female ambassadors mentor juniors 1-on-1.

### 3. Hackathon Squads & Imagine Cup Incubation
- Proactively forming and coaching all-female and gender-balanced teams for national hackathons and the prestigious **Microsoft Imagine Cup**.
- Providing architectural guidance, pitch deck design, and presentation rehearsals to build stage confidence.

### 4. Industry Mentorship & Executive Tech Talks
- Hosting fireside chats and career panels featuring accomplished female software engineers, engineering managers, and Microsoft MVPs from top tech companies.
- Demystifying technical interview processes, portfolio development, and navigating corporate engineering cultures.

## 🛡️ Safe Space & Harassment Zero-Tolerance Policy

Her Tech Wing is dedicated to ensuring unconditional psychological safety:
1. **Zero Tolerance:** Any harassment, condescension, dismissive behavior, or inappropriate communication directed toward female members—whether in person, in computer labs, or over online groups—results in **immediate expulsion** from the chapter and referral to the FAST-NUCES Campus Disciplinary Committee.
2. **Confidential Reporting:** Female students may report concerns directly and confidentially to **VP Asawir binte Asif** or **Faculty Advisor Sir Samin Ahmed** without fear of academic or social retaliation.

## 🚀 How to Get Involved

- **Join the Network:** Open to all enrolled female undergraduates across CS, SE, AI, Data Science, and Electrical Engineering at FAST-NUCES Peshawar.
- **Become a Mentor:** Senior female students (3rd and 4th year) who have completed internships or major cloud projects are encouraged to serve as Circle Mentors.
- **Connect:** Reach out via official channels or directly contact the Her Tech Wing coordinator via `msp.pwr@nu.edu.pk`.
