# Communication & External Relations Policy

**Microsoft Student Ambassadors — FAST-NUCES Peshawar**  
*Internal Communication Matrix, External Representation, & Official Email Templates*

---

## 📡 Purpose & Scope

Clear, professional, and reliable communication protects the institutional reputation of **FAST-NUCES Peshawar** and ensures flawless operational coordination across our executive council, departmental wings, and community members.

This policy defines:
1. The **Communication Channel Matrix** (which tool to use for which purpose).
2. **Official Representation Protocols** (who speaks on behalf of the chapter).
3. **Standardized Email Templates** for university administration, sponsors, speakers, and student inductions.

---

## 🗂️ 1. Communication Channel Matrix

To prevent communication fragmentation, our operations follow a strict hierarchy of tools:

| Channel | Primary Audience | Permitted Usage | Prohibited Usage |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Official Email**<br/>`msp.pwr@nu.edu.pk` | University Admin, Corporate Sponsors, Speakers, Microsoft PMs | Formal administrative requests, sponsorship proposals, speaker invitations, formal dispute notices. | Casual conversation, internal memes, unreviewed drafts. |
| **WhatsApp Core Group** | Executive Council & Core Body Members | Urgent meeting alerts, day-of-event logistics, operational announcements, rapid polling. | Lengthy code reviews, multi-page technical debates, sharing sensitive credentials. |
| **WhatsApp Community** | Registered FAST Students & Attendees | Workshop announcements, registration links, event updates, community Q&A. | Spam, non-technical promotions, unsolicited private messaging of members. |
| **Discord Server** | Developers, Designers, Study Groups | Asynchronous technical discussions, code debugging, pairing sessions, Her Tech Wing study jams. | Final administrative sign-offs, formal policy decisions. |
| **GitHub Issues & PRs** | Technical Team & Open Source Contributors | Code review discussions, architectural RFCs, bug reports, feature task division. | Non-technical society gossip, personal scheduling conflicts. |
| **LinkedIn & Instagram** | Public Tech Community, Industry, Alumni | Event announcements, workshop photo dumps, recap reels, chapter achievements, sponsor thank-yous. | Unapproved controversial opinions, unofficial personal side projects. |

## 🏛️ 2. External Representation & Press Protocol

### 2.1 Authorized Spokespersons
Only the following individuals are authorized to make formal statements, sign agreements, or represent **MSA FAST-NUCES Peshawar** to university leadership, external companies, news outlets, or Microsoft representatives:
1. **Faculty Advisor:** Sir Samin Ahmed
2. **President:** Rayyan Shaheer
3. **Vice Presidents:** Subhan Baig & Asawir binte Asif (or an Outreach Lead with explicit written presidential delegation)

### 2.2 Mandatory Email Inclusions (CC Rules)
Whenever an official email is sent to FAST-NUCES campus administration, corporate sponsors, or guest speakers:
- The email must originate from the official address: `msp.pwr@nu.edu.pk`.
- **Mandatory CC:** Both the **Faculty Advisor** (`samin.ahmed@nu.edu.pk`) and the **President** must be included in the `CC` line.
- The General Secretary logs all dispatched correspondence in the society administrative register.
