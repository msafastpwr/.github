## 🔤 3. Typography & Font Hierarchy

Clear typography ensures legibility on projection screens, printed flyers, and mobile displays:

### 3.1 Font Families
- **Display & Title Font:** **Segoe UI** (Semi-bold / Bold). If unavailable, fall back to **Inter** or **Helvetica Neue**.
- **Body & Editorial Font:** **Inter** or **Segoe UI** (Regular / 400).
- **Code & Technical Monospace:** **Cascadia Code**, **Consolas**, or **Fira Code**.

### 3.2 Type Scale (Digital & Print)

| Level | Size (px / pt) | Weight | Line Height | Application |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Display 1** | 48–64 px | Bold (700) | 1.1 | Event hero titles, main poster headlines |
| **Heading 1** | 32–40 px | Semi-bold (600) | 1.25 | Section headers, slide titles |
| **Heading 2** | 24–28 px | Semi-bold (600) | 1.3 | Card titles, speaker names, subheadings |
| **Heading 3** | 18–20 px | Medium (500) | 1.4 | Module topics, metadata headers |
| **Body Large** | 16–18 px | Regular (400) | 1.6 | Lead paragraphs, workshop descriptions |
| **Body Normal**| 14–15 px | Regular (400) | 1.5 | Standard copy, body text, bullet points |
| **Caption/Tag**| 12–13 px | Medium (500) | 1.4 | Badges, date/time chips, footnotes |

## 🛡️ 4. Logo Usage & Clear Space Rules

### 4.1 Clear Space
Always maintain an exclusion zone around the official MSA logo equal to at least half the height of the logo symbol (`0.5H`). No text, graphics, or borders may encroach within this zone.

```
       ┌───────────────────────────────┐
       │             0.5 H             │
       │   0.5 H  [ MSA LOGO ]  0.5 H  │
       │             0.5 H             │
       └───────────────────────────────┘
```

### 4.2 Minimum Dimensions
- **Digital Displays:** Minimum height of **32 pixels**.
- **Print Materials:** Minimum height of **12 millimeters (0.5 inches)** to prevent degradation of thin geometry.

### 4.3 Unacceptable Manipulations (Strictly Forbidden)
- ❌ **Do NOT stretch, squish, or distort** the aspect ratio of the logo.
- ❌ **Do NOT recolor** the logo in random gradient colors or unapproved neon tones.
- ❌ **Do NOT add heavy drop shadows, glows, or bevels** to the logo mark.
- ❌ **Do NOT place the logo over busy, high-contrast photographic backgrounds** without an opaque scrim or semi-transparent dark backing card.
- ❌ **Do NOT rotate or tilt** the logo.

## 📱 5. Social Media & Digital Asset Dimensions

Always design promotional graphics to the exact native aspect ratios to prevent platform cropping:

| Format / Platform | Resolution | Aspect Ratio | Safe Zone & Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Instagram / LinkedIn Square** | `1080 x 1080 px` | 1:1 | Center primary content; keep text 80px away from borders. |
| **Instagram Portrait Feed** | `1080 x 1350 px` | 4:5 | Ideal for speaker flyers; maximized vertical screen real estate. |
| **Instagram / WhatsApp Story** | `1080 x 1920 px` | 9:16 | Keep top 250px and bottom 250px clear for system UI / avatars. |
| **LinkedIn Page Banner** | `1584 x 396 px` | 4:1 | Keep essential logo and text centered; right side is preferred. |
| **YouTube / Presentation Slide**| `1920 x 1080 px` | 16:9 | Full HD widescreen standard for all workshop presentation decks. |
| **Website Open Graph (OG)** | `1200 x 630 px` | 1.91:1 | Banner preview when URLs are shared on WhatsApp, LinkedIn, X. |

## 📄 6. Event Posters & Certificate Standards

### 6.1 Mandatory Elements for Event Posters
Every official event flyer must include:
1. **Institutional Lockup:** FAST-NUCES Peshawar Logo (top-left or top-right) and MSA FAST Peshawar Logo.
2. **Clear Event Title:** Large, bold headline describing the topic (e.g., *Mastering Git & GitHub: Branching to Deployment*).
3. **Speaker Details:** Professional portrait photo, speaker's full name, role/title, and organization/company.
4. **Date, Time & Venue:** Explicit details (e.g., *Wednesday, 18th November 2026 • 2:00 PM – 4:00 PM • CS Computer Lab 1*).
5. **Registration QR Code & Short Link:** Clean, high-contrast QR code linking to the Microsoft Form RSVP.
6. **Partner Logos (if co-hosted):** Partner club logos must be sized harmoniously.
7. **Social & Contact Footer:** Website (`msafastpwr.github.io`) and `@msafastpwr` handles.

### 6.2 Official Certificate Standards
- **Resolution:** Minimum 300 DPI (print ready) at A4 size (`3508 x 2480 px`).
- **Required Fields:**
  - Full Name of Recipient (dynamically rendered from verified attendance list).
  - Specific Achievement: *"For active participation in..."* or *"In recognition of meritorious service as..."*.
  - Event Date and Academic Year.
  - Dual Signatures: **President (Rayyan Shaheer)** and **Faculty Advisor (Sir Samin Ahmed)**.
  - Unique Certificate Serial / Verification Hash linking to the chapter online verification system.

## ✍️ 7. Tone of Voice & Editorial Style

### 7.1 How We Speak
- **Professional, Yet Accessible:** We write with technical precision while remaining warm and welcoming to newcomers.
- **Empowering & Action-Oriented:** We inspire students to build. Use verbs like *Build, Deploy, Architect, Innovate, Explore, Ship*.
- **Accurate & Modest:** We avoid exaggerated clickbait. Describe workshops truthfully (e.g., say *"Hands-on introduction to Azure Functions"* rather than *"Become an AI God in 2 Hours"*).
