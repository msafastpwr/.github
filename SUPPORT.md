# Support & Community Help

Welcome to the **MSA FAST-NUCES Peshawar** support page! We are here to help you get the assistance, mentorship, and resources you need.

---

## 🧭 Where to Seek Help

Depending on your question or need, please use the most appropriate channel below:

### 1. Technical Issues & Code Inquiries (GitHub)
- **Bug Reports & Issues:** If you encounter a bug on our website ([`msafastpwr.github.io`](https://github.com/msafastpwr/msafastpwr.github.io)) or any chapter project, please open a GitHub Issue in the respective repository using our [Bug Report Template](.github/ISSUE_TEMPLATE/bug_report.yml).
- **Feature & Topic Proposals:** Have an idea for a feature or want to request a specific workshop topic? Use our [Workshop Request Template](.github/ISSUE_TEMPLATE/workshop_request.yml) or [Feature Request Template](.github/ISSUE_TEMPLATE/feature_request.yml).

### 2. General Community & Workshop Discussions (WhatsApp)
- For fast-paced discussions, real-time workshop announcements, queries about upcoming sessions, and peer networking, join our official student community:
  👉 **[Join the MSA FAST PWR WhatsApp Community](https://chat.whatsapp.com/LoL1SKxYWUQ97wsRcZ0qcW)**

### 3. Formal & Institutional Inquiries (Email)
- For university administration, society collaborations, sponsorships, or official inquiries:
  📧 **Email:** [msp.pwr@nu.edu.pk](mailto:msp.pwr@nu.edu.pk)

### 4. Social Media Channels
