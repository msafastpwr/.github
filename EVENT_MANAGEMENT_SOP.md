# Event Management Standard Operating Procedure (SOP)

**Microsoft Student Ambassadors — FAST-NUCES Peshawar**  
*End-to-End Operational Lifecycle for On-Campus Workshops, Bootcamps, & Flagship Events*

---

## 📋 Executive Overview

Delivering seamless, high-impact technical workshops and community events requires meticulous planning, strict adherence to FAST-NUCES administrative policies, and disciplined day-of execution.

This Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) defines the mandatory timeline, responsibilities, approval workflows, and checklists for every event conducted under the **MSA FAST Peshawar** banner.

## 🏛️ Phase 1: Inception, Budgeting & Campus Approvals (T-14 to T-10 Days)

No event may be publicly announced before formal administrative clearance is secured.

### 1.1 Event Concept Note Formulation
- The organizing lead drafts an **Event Concept Document** covering:
  - Event Title & Technical Abstract.
  - Learning Outcomes & Alignment with Microsoft technologies (Azure, AI, GitHub, Power Platform).
  - Target Audience (Freshmen, Sophomores, All Campus, Inter-University).
  - Expected Attendee Count & Venue Choice (Computer Lab 1/2, Main Auditorium, Video Conference Room).
  - Speaker Profile (Internal Lead, FAST Faculty, Alumni, or External Microsoft MVP).

### 1.2 Budgeting & Financial Approval
- If funds are needed (guest mementos, speaker travel, printed certificates, high-tea refreshments):
  - Draft an itemized expense estimation spreadsheet.
  - Submit to the President and Faculty Advisor for review.
  - Identify funding source: Corporate sponsorship, departmental society allocation, or self-sustaining registration fee (if approved).

### 1.3 FAST-NUCES Formal Permission Protocol
- **Step 1:** Draft the formal permission request letter addressed to the **Director / Head of Computer Science Department**.
- **Step 2:** Obtain the physical/digital signature and endorsement from **Faculty Advisor (Sir Samin Ahmed)**.
- **Step 3:** Submit to the Campus Administration Office / Academic Office for venue reservation and register on the official campus master event calendar.
- **Step 4:** Send formal notification to Campus Security and IT Support detailing required gate passes for external guests and lab requisitions.

## 📢 Phase 2: Promotional Campaign & Registration (T-10 to T-3 Days)

Coordinated by the **Outreach**, **Graphics**, and **Media** teams.

### 2.1 Visual Assets & Design Approval
- **T-10 Days:** Graphics Team receives confirmed event copy, speaker photo, date, time, and venue.
- **T-8 Days:** Graphics Team submits poster drafts adhering to [`BRAND_GUIDELINES.md`](BRAND_GUIDELINES.md).
- **T-7 Days:** Final review and sign-off by the President and Graphics Lead.

### 2.2 Registration Setup
- Create an official registration form on **Microsoft Forms**:
  - Required fields: Full Name, Student Roll Number, Degree Program & Batch, University Email, WhatsApp Number, Prior Experience level, Personal Laptop availability.
  - Set response limit capped at **120% of venue capacity** (factoring in standard 15–20% student drop-off).

### 2.3 Coordinated Marketing Blitz
- **T-6 Days (Teaser & Registration Launch):**
  - Publish primary poster on LinkedIn and Instagram.
  - Dispatch official announcement to the WhatsApp Community Group.
  - Add event card to chapter website (`msafastpwr.github.io/events/`).
- **T-4 Days (Classroom Announcements):**
  - Outreach ambassadors conduct brief 2-minute announcements at the start/end of designated computer science lecture halls with professor permission.
- **T-3 Days (Speaker Spotlight):**
  - Release dedicated speaker background card detailing professional credentials and topic deep dive.
- **T-1 Day (Confirmation Notice):**
  - Send email/WhatsApp confirmation to accepted attendees with prerequisites (e.g., *"Please install VS Code and Git prior to tomorrow's lab"*).

## 🧪 Phase 3: Technical & Venue Readiness (T-2 Days to T-2 Hours)

Coordinated by the **Technical** and **Events** teams.

### 3.1 Computer Lab & Network Validation (T-48 Hours)
- Visit the reserved computer lab in person:
  - Check lab PC availability and working condition.
  - Verify that the university firewall does not block critical developer endpoints (GitHub.com, npm registry, Azure Portal, Docker Hub).
  - Verify Wi-Fi signal strength in the room for participants bringing personal laptops.
  - Prepare a shared USB flash drive containing offline installers for VS Code, Git, Python, and workshop starter code repositories in case of unexpected network degradation.

### 3.2 Audio / Visual Testing (T-24 Hours)
- Test the projector with the speaker's laptop (verify both HDMI and USB-C adapters).
- Test projector aspect ratio (16:9 widescreen) and color contrast against whiteboard glare.
- Test wireless handheld microphones and collar mics; check amplifier gain to prevent acoustic feedback.
- Confirm extension cords and power multi-plugs are placed along desks for attendee laptops.

### 3.3 Procurement & Stationery Check (T-24 Hours)
- Verify printed materials:
  - Attendee sign-in roster sheets.
  - Badges/lanyards for guest speakers and executive team.
  - Official framed memento / shield for guest speaker.
- Confirm refreshment delivery schedule with campus cafeteria or caterer.

## 🚀 Phase 4: Event Day Operations & Execution (T-0)

### 4.1 Chronological Day-of-Event Timeline

| Time Delta | Milestone | Responsible Team | Actions |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **T - 60 min** | Team Assembly | Entire Core Body | Arrive at venue. Check physical room arrangement, podium, seating rows. |
| **T - 45 min** | Tech Dry-Run | Technical Team | Connect speaker laptop, test slide clicker, verify mic audio levels. |
| **T - 30 min** | Desk Setup | Events Team | Setup Registration Desk at entrance with sign-in sheets and QR scanners. |
| **T - 20 min** | Doors Open | Events Team | Greet attendees, verify roll numbers, guide students to front rows first. |
| **T - 10 min** | Speaker Briefing | President & VPs | Escort guest speaker to green room / front row; review agenda and time cues. |
| **T - 00 min** | Opening Address | Host / MC | Welcome attendees, introduce society mission, acknowledge Faculty Advisor. |
| **T + 05 min** | Code of Conduct | Host / MC | Brief reminder of respectful participation and psychological safety. |
| **T + 10 min** | Keynote / Workshop | Speaker | Session delivery. Tech team actively patrols aisles to unblock student bugs. |
| **T + 75 min** | Interactive Q&A | Host & Speaker | Facilitate questions from the audience, ensuring diverse student voices. |
| **T + 90 min** | Memento & Photos | Executive Council | Present memento to speaker. Invite Faculty Advisor for closing remarks. |
| **T + 95 min** | Official Group Photo | Media Team | Stage all attendees for the official wide-angle chapter group photograph. |
| **T + 100 min**| Refreshments | Events Team | Systematic refreshment distribution avoiding crowded exits. |
| **T + 120 min**| Hall Cleanup | Events Team | Inspect lab; ensure all trash is cleared, PCs logged off, projector powered down. |

## 📊 Phase 5: Post-Event Reporting & Wrap-Up (T+1 to T+3 Days)

An event is not concluded when the lights turn off. Post-event execution solidifies our community standing:

### 5.1 Media Production & Social Recap (Within 24 Hours)
- Media Team color-grades top 10 photos and exports a 60-second highlight reel.
- Outreach Team drafts and publishes the official LinkedIn and Instagram recap posts:
  - Tag speaker, FAST-NUCES, Microsoft Learn Student Ambassadors, and attending student societies.
  - Include summary metrics (e.g., *"Over 80+ students joined us to master Azure Cloud..."*).

### 5.2 Feedback Collection & Analytics (Within 24 Hours)
- Send digital feedback survey link via email/WhatsApp to all attendees.
- Measure: Content Clarity, Speaker Effectiveness, Lab Hand-on Utility, Net Promoter Score (NPS).

### 5.3 Digital Certificate Issuance (Within 72 Hours)
- Cross-reference registration data against verified physical sign-in sheets.
- Generate verified certificates containing unique serial hashes and dual signatures.
- Distribute via email using the chapter automated dispatch script.

### 5.4 Financial Reconciliation & Archive (Within 5 Days)
- General Secretary compiles all original purchase receipts and invoices.
- Submit financial expenditure report to Faculty Advisor and Campus Accounts.
- Archive workshop slides, code solutions, and recordings into the public repository under `github.com/msafastpwr`.

## 🚨 Emergency Contingency Protocols

| Failure Mode | Trigger Scenario | Immediate Action Protocol |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Projector / Display Failure** | Projector lamp dies or HDMI handshake fails. | 1. Switch immediately to backup HDMI cable.<br/>2. If projector dead, migrate session to the auxiliary TV screens or switch to interactive whiteboard walk-through.<br/>3. Tech team notifies campus IT hotline. |
| **Campus Internet Outage** | Wi-Fi drops or DNS fails across the lab. | 1. Direct attendees to connect to pre-configured mobile 4G hotspot hubs.<br/>2. Tech team distributes the offline USB installer packs prepared in Phase 3.<br/>3. Speaker shifts to local offline development / presentation slides. |
| **Speaker Delay / Cancellation** | Speaker stuck in traffic or faces medical emergency. | 1. If delayed <20 min: Lead initiates an impromptu Git/GitHub lighting challenge or icebreaker session.<br/>2. If cancelled: Technical Lead steps in to deliver pre-approved backup presentation deck on cloud fundamentals. |
| **Severe Overcrowding** | 50+ walk-in students beyond lab seating capacity. | 1. Prioritize registered attendees on the verified RSVP list.<br/>2. Offer unseated walk-ins priority reservation for the upcoming duplicate session.<br/>3. Strictly never violate campus fire and safety codes by blocking lab aisles. |
